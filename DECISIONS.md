# Decisions

- Start years are 1995, 2005, and 2026. A year is 52 weeks. Advancing a month moves four weeks.
- Saves are JSON documents in Postgres, one per slot (five slots). The catalog stays in normalized tables. The engine never imports Prisma.
- Hidden traits are hashed from the TMDB id so they stay stable across saves. The roster only shows hints the scouts or verdicts have revealed.
- People with no birthday, or who died before the start year, are not eligible. "Today" also excludes anyone with a death date.
- Genre mix comes from `known_for` plus a cached set of popular and top-rated films, because person credits do not include genre ids. Romantic Comedy is added when Comedy and Romance co-occur. Superhero, Sports, and Biopic are game genres only.
- The player starts with an empty roster, $400,000, and three rivals who already represent 36 popular eligible actors. About 40 scored NPC films exist so the first awards season has nominees. World projects never attach the player's clients; that work arrives as offers.
- Commission is collected when photography starts, including each new season. Post-production and airing do not block the calendar. A personal hold does.
- A lapsed or released client stays in the save as unsigned, so verdicts, stats, and awards survive. Rivals are a separate agency. Re-signing the same person keeps that file.
- Representation can be non-exclusive. Jobs are still commissioned while the actor is signed. Non-exclusive clients are easier to poach.
- Gender 1 and 2 map to actress and actor categories. Unspecified genders compete in an open performance category.
- Studios, streamers, and review outlets are fictional. Ceremonies use their real names. The critics prize is the Critics Circle Awards. The booby prize is the Golden Raspberry Awards.
- An unresolved inbox event pauses "+1 week" and "until next event". The five-year simulator auto-resolves them.
- Binge series record every episode's viewership on the premiere week. Weekly series reveal one episode per turn.
- A few TMDB people have no portrait. The seed keeps paging until at least 2,000 actors and 300 directors have image paths. Anyone still missing a photo renders as initials.
- Opening staff list is empty. Scout level reveals that many trait hints per client each week.
- Each actor has a primary medium (Film, TV, or Both), separate film and TV star power, and a prestige score per medium. With cached credits, the medium follows the share, quality, and recency of movie credits versus TV credits, plus popularity. Star power is a log of that credit mass, so a long filmography does not pin every famous actor at 99, and a small id tint keeps film-only actors from sharing one TV number. With no credits, it is a stable hash of the person id so old fixtures and old saves still load. TV regulars lean TV, film stars lean Film, and a strong slate in both is Both. Already-saved star numbers are left alone.
- Series quotes are per episode. The offer shows the episode fee, the episode count, the total, and the season number. Lead and series regular block the whole prep-and-shoot window. Recurring books about half the episodes as one or two discrete blocks. Guest stars book one or two episodes. Blocks never overlap for the same actor. A personal hold, a franchise hold, and a guaranteed multi-season hold are hard blocks. A pilot hold is a status through the decision date: the actor can take work in the gaps, and a pickup releases anything that collides with the series.
- Film stars are quoted a large premium for series work and will not take a 12-episode-or-longer lead or regular order. The warning is on the offer. TV stars start with lower film pull; film star power rises on hits and drops on flops, more so on tentpoles. Both-medium actors get an extra chance at an offer each week. Those extra rolls use a side RNG so the main weekly random stream stays stable.
- Leads and series regulars on ongoing shows can sign a one-season deal, a studio option, or a 3, 5, 6, or 7 season guarantee with an annual bump. A guarantee locks the later calendar and caps the quote. Options leave the off-season open and can be dropped. Limited series, miniseries, anthologies, recurring roles, and guests are one season. When a guaranteed deal is cancelled, the agency commissions 40 percent of the remaining seasons. When a player client is up for renewal, the inbox gets a re-sign, counter, or walk offer. Guaranteed years still left continue automatically at the bumped fee.
- Pilot season is weeks 8 through 20. New ongoing dramas and sitcoms can start as pilots in that window; limited series and most streaming shows are straight to series. A pilot pays a pilot fee, blocks a two-week shoot, and attaches the series option up front. After the pilot the network picks it up (about 46 percent), passes (about 38 percent), or retools, and a retool recasts about half the time. The profile shows Pilot, awaiting decision, with the decision date, and On hold (pilot) while the option is open.
- A series page lists every series with status, genre, network, and year. The series itself shows each season's production year, release year, episodes, viewership, critics, renewal, and cast with role and episode count. Actor profiles keep a series history (role, seasons, years, episodes, earnings) and film credits with year, role, gross, and reviews.
- Tentpoles and studio films scale the upfront fee with film star power. Indie and micro-indie work pays less and protects awards chances. A-list and Icon tentpoles can add bonuses at 2x and 3x budget, and at least 5 or 8 backend points. A hit tentpole can lock two to four later films at rising fees; those dates sit on the calendar until the picture is offered. A tentpole flop also cuts buzz and reputation. Agency cash on the agency page is split into film, series, pilots, and bonuses.
- Autosave runs once per in-game week, in the same transaction as the turn, including every week of a multi-week jump. Signing, negotiating, and the other office actions still write the open slot so a reload does not drop the signature, but they do not move Last saved and they do not toast. The header shows Last saved with the in-game date. Save now lives in the Game menu. A failed save shows an error and Retry only. Save slots, Continue, the RNG, and the multi-tab version check stay. Pending offers, pilot holds, and approaches live on the weekly state.
